Frequently Asked Questions

Fitness Nutrition FAQs

How much protein do I need?

Generally 1.2–2.2 g per kg of body weight depending on your activity and goals.

Can I lose weight without exercise?

Yes, a calorie deficit is the primary driver of weight loss, though exercise supports health and muscle retention.

How much water should I drink?

Most adults benefit from about 2.5–4 litres daily, adjusting for climate and activity level.

Is rice unhealthy?

No. Rice can be part of a balanced diet when eaten in appropriate portions.
